## H-bridge
![H-bridge](https://github.com/Seasoning3/Cluster/blob/main/img/h-bridge.jpeg)
An H-bridge is an electronic circuit that switches the polarity of a voltage applied to a load.  
These circuits are often used to allow the direction of a motor to be reversed using a single voltage.  
This enables control of the motor’s forward and reverse rotation.

H-bridge Table  
| Direction | motor1PinA | motor1PinB |
|---------|------|-----|
| Forward | HIGH | LOW |
| Backward | LOW | HIGH |
| Stop | LOW | LOW |
